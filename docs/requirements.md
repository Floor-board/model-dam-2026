# Requirements

## Functional requirements

Document the required operating outcomes for the dam model.

Examples:

- control and regulate water flow
- store a defined amount of water
- allow safe overflow via a spillway
- support a measurement or monitoring system
- maintain structural integrity under expected operating conditions

## Non-functional requirements

Examples:

- maintain safe working conditions
- use materials suitable for the intended load and water exposure
- keep the design easy to assemble and inspect
- include documentation for each revision
- preserve clarity for future maintenance or modification

## Design constraints

Record constraints such as:

- available space or footprint
- material availability
- budget limitations
- pumping or water source limitations
- permissible height or width
- local safety or environmental restrictions

## Performance targets

Examples:

- target storage volume
- target discharge rate
- target operating head
- maximum safe water level
- spillway capacity
- sensor or instrumentation accuracy requirements

## Safety requirements

Document expected safety provisions such as:

- overflow protection
- structural factor of safety
- electrical isolation or protection
- emergency shutoff or bypass routes
- monitoring of abnormal conditions

## Acceptance criteria

Define what success looks like for the project.

Example criteria:

- the model stores the target flow without overtopping under expected conditions
- outlet and spillway function as designed
- electrical controls operate reliably
- recorded measurements align with expected calculations

## Assumptions

List the assumptions that the design relies on.

Examples:

- inflow values are estimated from design conditions
- material properties follow standard values
- losses and inefficiencies are approximated
- no extreme weather or catastrophic events are considered in the baseline design

Update this document as requirements change or new performance goals are added.
